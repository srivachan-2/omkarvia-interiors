Our Story

Poloju Ravinder Chary

50 YEARS OF
EXPERIENCE

For nearly 50 years, our father (P. Ravinder Chary) has dedicated his life to this business with hard work, discipline, craftsmanship and honesty.

From childhood, we grew up watching his commitment to work, his respect for customers and his responsibility in every project. He did not simply teach us business with words. He taught us by example.

Inspired by his journey, we stepped into this field with a dream to carry forward his legacy.

Our father (P. Ravinder Chary) is our first teacher, our greatest role model and the foundation of our values.

With that inspiration, we established Omkar Integrated Projects Private Limited and our brand Omkarvia Interiors, combining traditional values with modern ideas, quality workmanship and professional execution.

This company is more than a business — it is our father's living legacy.
